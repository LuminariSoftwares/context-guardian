# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people, regardless of background or identity. We pledge that everyone participating in this community will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and perspectives
- Offering constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Harassing photography or recording without consent
- Sustained disruption of community spaces or discussions

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples include use of an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue titled `Conduct report: private contact requested` with no details in the issue body. A maintainer will contact you privately to discuss the report.

Do not post details about the incident publicly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

### Correction
Violations that do not align with community standards will result in private feedback and a request for corrective action.

### Warning
Serious violations will result in a written warning with clear expectations for future behavior.

### Temporary Ban
Repeated violations may result in a temporary ban from the community for a specified period.

### Permanent Ban
Persistent violations will result in permanent removal from the community.
